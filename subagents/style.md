# Ampixa Style

Source checked: https://ampixa.com/ on 2026-06-10.

Core voice:
- Text-first, plain, and technical.
- Monospace/minimal visual sensibility: white background, black text, crimson accent `#DC143C`, light grey separators.
- Talk about raw Nepali audio infrastructure: TTS, ASR data, forced alignment, pronunciation tooling, evaluation, lexicons, benchmarks, and regional speech work.
- Emphasize speech before text and speech after text becomes voice.
- Use Nepal, Nepali, regional languages, native listeners, messy audio, accent, breath, noise, code-switching, hesitation, dialect, and bad microphones when relevant.

Claims discipline:
- Do not overclaim national scale or model superiority unless there is benchmark evidence.
- Prefer "we built", "we measured", "we released", "we are testing", and "this improves the pipeline" over vague hype.
- Tie every public claim to an artifact: repo, dataset, model card, benchmark, demo, chart, screenshot, or human evaluation.

Visual assets:
- Declared site logo/fav icon: `https://ampixa.com/assets/logo.png`.
- Local copy: `assets/ampixa/logo.png`.
- Local wordmarks: `assets/ampixa/wordmark.svg` and `assets/ampixa/wordmark-dev.svg`.
- Source wordmarks on the site: `/assets/wordmark.svg` and `/assets/wordmark-dev.svg`.

Post shape:
- Lead with the concrete artifact.
- Explain why it matters for Nepali or regional speech infrastructure.
- Mention one technical detail that proves the work is real.
- End with a low-pressure prompt only when useful.
